☀️ Sunshine — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

In short

Sunshine is a personal habit, wellbeing and study tracker. There is no Sunshine server. Your information stays on your own device, and, only if you choose to sign in with Google, in a private folder of your own Google Drive.

What Sunshine stores

  • Your first name, an optional trusted person's name and phone number, your routine, habits, moods, notes, study sessions, comfort links, settings and photos you add.
  • Your PIN is never stored as typed. Only a salted, one-way hash (PBKDF2) is kept on your device, and it is never uploaded anywhere.

Where it is stored

  • On your device, inside the app's own storage.
  • In your Google Drive (optional). If you choose “Continue with Google”, Sunshine saves one backup file in Drive's hidden application data folder, using the drive.appdata permission. Only Sunshine can see this folder. Sunshine cannot see, open or change any of your other Drive files.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, Sunshine receives your name and email address (to greet you and show which account is connected) and access to its own app data folder in your Drive. This data is used only to save and restore your Sunshine data across your devices. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or transferred to anyone. The developer has no access to your Google account or your Drive. Sunshine's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What Sunshine does not do

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • No account with the developer. The developer never receives your data.

Notifications and permissions (Android)

Reminders are scheduled on your phone. Optional “focus lock” uses Android app pinning during a study session and can be ended at any time. Nothing about these leaves your device.

Your choices

  • Sign out of Google any time in Me → Google Drive.
  • Delete everything on the device with Erase everything. To remove the Drive copy, sign out and remove Sunshine's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions (choose “Delete hidden app data” if offered).
  • Export a backup any time from Me.

Children

Sunshine is not directed at children under 13.
